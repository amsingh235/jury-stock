Placeholder summary. Graham separates investing from speculation and argues for a margin of safety: buy only when price sits well below a conservative estimate of intrinsic value. Mr. Market offers prices daily; the investor may ignore him or exploit his mood swings. Defensive investors favor large, conservatively financed firms with long dividend records and moderate price-to-earnings and price-to-book ratios.
